Medico-Legal Services

We intervene by assessing and reporting on the functional levels of clients affected by injury, accident, or medical negligence.

Independent assessments and reports for legal matters, provided from private hospitals in Gauteng and at external locations throughout South Africa.
Prenatal – 12 yrs

Paediatrics

We intervene with child development, learning, and functional difficulties, facilitating success at home, school, and beyond.

Play-based assessment and intervention for milestones, school readiness, handwriting, sensory processing and daily independence, with parents involved every step. Free screenings currently offered for children under 7 with a developmental delay.
Adolescents & adults

Adolescents and Adults

Equipping youth and adults with life skills, emotional regulatory skills, and functional skills through purposeful activities.

Individual sessions for stress, burnout, mental-health recovery and life transitions. Practical, activity-based, and centred on what matters to you.
All ages

Group Based Intervention

Exploring social skills and addressing challenges in a group setting.

Structured groups that practise communication, conflict resolution and connection in real time, guided by an occupational therapist. Weekly sessions every Friday, 13:30–15:00, led by professionals who specialise in neurodivergence and people with lived experience.
Working adults

Work Based Intervention

Ensuring the best fit between work and person, prioritizing employee wellness for individuals and corporate companies.

Workplace assessments, return-to-work planning and wellness programmes that keep people functioning well in the roles that matter to them.
After injury or illness

Full Functional Capacity Evaluation

Comprehensive standardized assessment evaluating the impact of injury or illness on physical, psychosocial, or cognitive symptoms.

An objective, evidence-based picture of current functional ability, and often the first step in planning rehabilitation or a return to work.

Common questions

Our rooms are at Life Carstenview Hospital, 181 Bekker Road, Vorna Valley, Midrand. Telehealth sessions are also available, and medico-legal assessments can be arranged throughout South Africa.
We start with a conversation and an assessment: understanding you, your context and the activities that matter in your daily life. From there we agree on goals and a plan together.
You're welcome to contact us directly. We'll let you know if anything else is needed for your situation, and we work alongside doctors, schools and employers where helpful.
Fees depend on the service and assessment involved. Contact us on WhatsApp or email and we'll gladly talk you through costs and medical-aid options before you commit to anything.
For many concerns, especially mental-health and workplace support, telehealth works well and removes travel barriers. Some assessments do need to happen in person; we'll advise you honestly when you book.
