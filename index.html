Our philosophy

Occupation at Core

Occupational Therapists believe that occupation, or purposeful activity, is at the core of health and well-being. It is through occupation that people can draw meaning from life within their respective contexts. The focus on mental health was inspired by the gap in regional, national and international platforms. The aim to enhance, enable and empower its clients is central to Bikitsha OT's daily practice, working with people and communities to meet their needs inter- and intra-personally, in consideration that the person who holds the problem also holds the solution.

Our vision

Empowering and enriching quality of life

To empower and to enrich the quality of life of our clients nationally and internationally, through client-centred health care, in keeping with the 4IR and health developments overall.

Our mission

World-class, holistic intervention

To provide world-class services for our clients, enhancing the value of their daily living through holistic intervention. We strive to provide high-quality health care and health support, using Occupational Therapy and mental-health knowledge to empower people from different communities, nations and parts of the world.

Since our inception

From face-to-face to full telehealth

Since its inception the company has been dedicated to client-centred services within paediatrics, mental health and corporate wellness. The shift from the traditional face-to-face set-up to telehealth has been challenging yet successful. We now boast fully functional telehealth services, while continuing to welcome clients daily to safe, regulatory-compliant offices.

Full Functional Capacity Evaluation

A standardised, comprehensive assessment of the physical, psychosocial and cognitive impact of injury or illness.
