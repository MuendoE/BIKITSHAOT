About Us

Where holistic well-being is the centre of focus.

Who we are

Client-centred, since 2015

Bikitsha Occupational Therapy is an Occupational Therapy practice that has centred its services in mental health, work-practice and medico-legal services. It was established by Nondumiso Irene Bikitsha, who has been working within the private practice sector since 2015.

What drives us

Unleashing full potential

Bikitsha Occupational Therapy is where holistic well-being is the centre of focus. We aspire to empower our clients to reach their full potential. Our focus as a business is holistic wellness intervention aimed at capacitating our clients: stimulating, celebrating, nurturing and enhancing your workforce to catalyse the eco-system that supports them.

How we work

Assessment and intervention

Occupational therapy uses assessment and intervention to develop, recover, or maintain the meaningful activities, or occupations, of people and groups. From paediatrics to corporate wellness, everything we do starts with the activities that give your life meaning.
